## Web app (flowbook/web)
React + Vite, plain CSS, works from phone width up. `cd flowbook/web && npm install && npm run dev` (port 5300; it proxies `/v1` to the API on 5200, so start the API too). In production serve `dist/` and `/v1` from one host. The product name is one constant (`src/lib.js` BRAND) plus `index.html`.
Screens: sign in and sign up, set up a business, home (what is owed, profit, books check), sales invoices, purchase bills and credit notes (draft, issue, void, apply, print), payments with matching, customers, vendors, items, chart of accounts, journals (manual entry, reverse), reports (profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance, who owes you / you owe, checks), settings (business details, GST rates, closing periods, team, FlowXP link with one-time secret and event replay, activity log). Not yet: sharing the FlowXP login, PDF download (Print uses the browser), bank statements, GST returns.

### Look and feel
The web app uses FlowXP's design system (`design.md`): same tokens (ink navy, logo blue, flat white grounds), Plus Jakarta Sans, 8/12/16 radii, borders over shadows, the same sidebar-plus-sticky-header shell, light and dark by the system setting. Motion is the FlowXP set: a short fade-and-rise on each page, staggered stat tiles, a lift on button hover, panels that settle in; all off under reduced motion.

### Hardening (2026-10-29)
Invoice, bill, credit note and payment numbers restart each financial year (`INV-26-27-000001`, 16 characters, within the GST limit); the invoice prefix is set per organization (Settings, up to three characters) and each prefix is its own series. Ageing is worked out for the date chosen. Posted payments and their allocations are frozen by database triggers (void instead). FlowXP events are stored and answered at once and posted by a background worker (`INBOX_WORKER=false` to post inside the request instead); a cancellation that arrives before its sale waits and runs by itself when the sale posts. Organizations made before the Discount given account existed got it from migration 0005.

### GST returns, recurring documents and statements (2026-10-29)
Added after reading the MIT-licensed [Free-GST-Billing-Software](https://github.com/IamRamgarhia/Free-GST-Billing-Software) (copyright its authors; the GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B file layout, the portal checks and the number rules follow it, re-written for FlowBook's paise and ledger model).
- **GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B drafts** (`api/src/gstr.ts`, Reports, GST returns): worked out from issued invoices, credit notes and bills of a month. GSTR-1 has B2B, B2CL (above 1 lakh from 1 Aug 2024), B2CS, CDNR, the HSN summary and documents issued. A list of what the portal is known to reject comes with it (invalid GSTIN, number over 16 characters, rate not on the portal list, missing or short HSN, missing unit), and the download is blocked until the blocking ones are fixed. **Not verified against the GST portal.** Not built: credit notes to unregistered buyers (CDNUR), exports, SEZ, reverse charge, cess, GSTR-3B table 3.2, interest and late fee, filing.
- **Units (UQC)** on items and lines (migration 0006); the HSN table needs them.
- **Recurring invoices and bills** (`api/src/recurring.ts`, migration 0006, Recurring screen): a template makes a DRAFT on each due date (catches up missed periods, at most 12 per run, stops at its end date). A person still issues it. It runs when someone presses "Make due drafts now"; there is no timer yet.
- **Party statement** (`api/src/statement.ts`, Reports, Party statement): documents and payments with a running balance.
- **Amount in words** under every document total, and a **GST due dates** card on the home screen (monthly filers only).
- Left out on purpose: the reference's HSN-to-rate table. Its rates predate or only partly follow the September 2025 rate change, so it would suggest wrong rates. Seeded tax codes are still GST0/5/12/18/28; add a 40% code (and retire 12/28 for new dates) in Settings once the owner's CA confirms the rates in use.

### WhatsApp (2026-10-29)
Settings, WhatsApp. Two modes, plus off:
- **Open WhatsApp (LINK):** no setup. The Send and Remind buttons on an invoice, and Send receipt on a payment, open a click-to-chat address with the message ready; a person presses send.
- **Send from FlowBook (CLOUD):** FlowBook sends through the business's own Meta WhatsApp Cloud API number (`api/src/whatsapp.ts`), and can remind on its own: an hourly worker (`WHATSAPP_WORKER=false` turns it off) sends the unpaid-invoice reminder at the chosen days from the due date (default 3 days before, on the day, 7 and 15 days after). Each invoice gets each reminder once, only while money is pending.
- Needs three **Utility** templates approved in Meta Business Manager, named as in Settings, with exactly the text shown there (`TEMPLATE_BODY` in whatsapp.ts). The access token is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM); set `FLOWBOOK_SECRET_KEY` (32 random bytes, base64) in `api/.env` first or saving a token is refused.
- A customer or vendor can be marked "do not send WhatsApp" and is then skipped (and logged as skipped). Every message, sent or not, is in the log on the invoice and in Settings.
- **Not tested against WhatsApp's live service** (needs a real number and approved templates); tests use a stand-in sender. No delivery or read receipts yet (needs Meta's webhook), no reply handling.

### Expenses and CSV import (2026-10-29)
- **Expenses** (`api/src/expenses.ts`, Expenses screen): money spent and paid at once. One journal: the expense account and the GST you can claim back are debited, cash, bank, UPI or card is credited. It can be voided (reversed). The GST on expenses counts as credit in the GSTR-3B draft. Something paid later is a purchase bill. No receipt attachments yet, and the vendor is only a name in the note (not a party).
- **CSV import** (`api/src/import.ts`, `web/src/csv.js`, Import CSV on Customers, Vendors and Items): the browser reads the file, "Check" runs the real import and undoes it so the preview is exact, then "Import" saves the good rows and lists the rest with the line number. Columns are matched by name in any order (template download included); a unit can be a code or a name, GST can be `18` or `GST18`, a state can be a name or code. Up to 1000 rows per file. Opening balances are not imported.

### Printing, e-way bill, TDS/TCS and sales analysis (2026-10-29)
- **Business profile and printing** (`api/src/profile.ts`, migration 0008, Settings > Business and Printing): address, phone, email, bank, IFSC, UPI and terms; print choices (A4, A5 or 80 mm thermal, colour, heading, show HSN/unit/bank/terms). Customers and vendors have a billing and a delivery address. Press Print on an issued invoice, bill or credit note, or "Print receipt" on a payment: a separate sheet (`web/src/pages/Print.jsx`) is shown only on paper, with CGST/SGST or IGST, TCS, amount in words, bank/UPI, terms and a signature line.
- **E-way bill file** (`api/src/eway.ts`, "E-way bill" on an issued invoice): asks for distance, mode, vehicle and transporter, lists what is missing (GSTIN, PIN codes), and downloads the bulk-upload JSON. It is a file to upload, not an e-way bill, and has not been checked on the NIC portal.
- **TDS** (migration 0009, payment form): tax a customer deducted is kept as TDS receivable; tax you deduct from a vendor is TDS payable. The payment amount stays the cash that moved; cash plus TDS settles the documents. The statement and the books both use the full amount.
- **TCS** (invoice form): a percent of value plus GST, added to the total and owed as TCS payable; invoices only. Rates and sections shown in the pickers are a helper, not tax advice.
- **Sales analysis** (Reports): month-by-month sales and purchases, top customers with what they still owe, best-selling items.
- **Tax-inclusive prices:** tick "GST is in this price" on a line; the tax is carved out of what you typed, so the total is exactly that amount (migration 0010).
- **Opening balances** (Settings > Opening balances, migration 0011): what customers owed you and you owed vendors when you started. Posted against an "Opening balances" account, payable like a normal document, shown on statements, and kept out of GST returns and sales analysis.
- **Reverse charge** (tick it on a purchase bill, migration 0012): the vendor is owed the price only; the GST is credited as output tax and debited as input credit, so it nets to nothing in the books. GSTR-3B shows it in 3.1(d) and as credit. Not covered: self-invoices, payment vouchers, and rules on which supplies are eligible; your CA decides when to use it.
- **Bank reconciliation** (Money > Bank, migration 0013, `api/src/reconcile.ts`): import the bank's CSV (one signed Amount column, or Deposit/Withdrawal columns; day-first dates) for a bank, UPI or card account; the same file twice adds nothing. Each statement line is matched one-to-one with an entry in the books (same amount; the nearest one within 5 days is suggested), or you add the missing entry (a charge, interest) and it matches itself, or ignore it. The screen shows the closing balance the statement should show, and the difference once you type the real one. Not built: bank feeds, rules that categorise lines automatically, matching one line to several entries, saved "reconciled up to" locks.
- **Quotes and sales orders** (Sales > Quotes, Sales orders; migration 0014): documents that post nothing and are never owed. "Mark as sent" numbers them (QUO-/SO-), the customer can be marked accepted or declined, and a sent quote becomes a sales order or an invoice, a sales order an invoice. The new document is a draft with the same lines and a "Made from" link; the original shows "Turned into". Not built: partial invoicing of an order, expiry reminders, emailing a quote, a customer accept link.
- **Purchase orders** (Purchases > Purchase orders, migration 0015): same as quotes: numbered PO-… when sent, posts nothing, vendor accepted or declined, and "Make bill" turns it into a draft bill with the same lines.
