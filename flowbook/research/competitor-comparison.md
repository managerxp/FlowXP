## Where FlowBook can win
1. **The POS is already ours.** Every competitor except Vyapar makes the shop re-key or import sales. FlowXP already has invoices, credit notes, GST options, reverse charge, TDS/TCS, GSTR-1/3B JSON, accountant pack and day book. FlowBook turns that into a real ledger without a second data entry.
2. **Industry-aware chart of accounts and reports.** A cafe, a gym and a spare-parts workshop need different accounts and KPIs (food cost, membership deferred revenue, labour vs parts).
3. **Messaging built in.** FlowXP's WhatsApp module (bills, reminders, Rate Us) is live; statements and dues reminders reuse it.
4. **Price floor.** Zoho gives a free plan with GST reports; FlowBook must not charge for what FlowXP already gives free. Money is made on automation, multi-GSTIN, compliance volume and AI.

## Where FlowBook should not compete (yet)
Payroll statutory (PF, ESI, PT), TDS return filing, ERP depth, multi-country tax, bank-feed breadth. Say so plainly; Zoho and Tally are ahead.

## Honest risks
- The Free-plan anchor is strong; Zoho also includes accountant access.
- Accountants prefer Tally; they trust it for their audit work. Plan an accountant export (Tally XML, Excel) so FlowBook does not fight them.
