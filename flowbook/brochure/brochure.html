Projects, flats and contractors NEW

From the first slab to the last instalment

Projects & sites

Give each site a project. Tag bills, invoices and expenses to it and see money in, money out and profit for that site. Cost types such as Materials, Labour, Contractors and Equipment keep the split clear.

Flats & plots, sold in stages

Add units (A-101, Plot 7), sell to a buyer at an agreed price and split it into stages that total 100%. Raise each stage as a GST invoice when the work reaches it; a cancelled stage can be raised again.

Work orders & RA bills

Agree the work with a subcontractor or client: description, unit, quantity, rate. Each RA bill takes the measured total to date and bills only the new work. It will not let you go backwards or exceed the contract.

Retention held back

Set a retention % on a work order (say 5%) and every RA bill holds it automatically. A bill cannot be paid beyond what is payable now; the held amount stays visible until released.

Advances

Money paid or received ahead of a bill is kept as an advance and applied to the next bill. One card shows every advance and every retention held.

Payments due

One report of who owes you and whom you owe, by age, as of any date. Send reminders from the same screen.

Builder view: only what a site office needs

A third view beside Simple and Full shows Projects, flats, bills, payments and stock, and hides the rest. Switch to Full any time.

Example. Plastering contract with Sharma Contractors: 1,000 sq m internal at ₹100 and 500 sq m external at ₹200, 5% retention. First RA bill: 400 and 100 measured. FlowBook bills ₹60,000 and holds ₹3,000. Second bill: internal now at 700. It bills only the new 300 sq m.

Ask the assistant

“Who owes me money?” Answers come from your real figures. It only reads; it never changes your books.

Careful with your numbers

  • Every entry balances; the books check proves each figure agrees with its documents.
  • Issued invoices cannot be edited, only cancelled with a record. Closed periods stay closed.
  • Roles control who sees and does what; every change is logged.
  • Each business’s data is kept separate from every other.

Honest about its limits

  • GST returns and e-invoices are prepared as files for you or your CA to upload. FlowBook does not file for you.
  • Bank statements come in by CSV, not live bank feeds.
  • TDS on contractors (section 194C) is not deducted automatically yet.
  • No payroll yet. Screens are mainly in English.
  • GST rates in the ready list are the common ones; confirm with your CA.
